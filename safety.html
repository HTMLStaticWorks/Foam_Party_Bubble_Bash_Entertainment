The Safety Checklist

Every event follows a rigorous, non-negotiable inspection flow.

Drainage paths, outlet testing, slip mats, and fence gaps verified with your site contact.
Foam depth capped for toddlers, teen zones separated when requested, and audio kept hearing-safe.
We pause cannons in gusty wind, stash cables, and resume when surfaces are stable.
